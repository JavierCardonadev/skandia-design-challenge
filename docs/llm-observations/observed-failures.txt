Raw outputs: prompt-v1-haiku-5-5.txt and prompt-v2-haiku-5-5.txt in this folder.

   Input:  kit sample E1, the "clear need" case (product, channel and goal are stated).
   Output: no steps; one question asking for the client group. All six samples got
           questions and none got a plan. Every answer passed schema validation.
   Fix:    prompt v2 states when the model may proceed and that the client group is an
           assumption to record, not a reason to ask. The interface shows assumptions
           with a "Lo supusimos" tag so the person can correct them.

   Input:  kit samples E3 and E5.
   Output: facts such as "Producto: No se indica el producto exacto".
   Fix:    prompt v2 defines a fact as something known; unknown things are asked.

           shown. The two missing steps are added in order and labelled "Lo agregamos
           al plan porque un paso siguiente lo necesita." See
           live-plan-with-added-steps.jpg.
