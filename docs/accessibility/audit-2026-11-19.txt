ACCESSIBILITY AUDIT · 19 Nov 2026 · target: WCAG 2.2 level AA
Audited on the public preview, after the model-backed steps, the verdict, the progressive
text and the history were in place.

METHOD
1. Automated: axe-core 4.10 (rules wcag2a, wcag2aa, wcag21a, wcag21aa, wcag22aa and
   best-practice) on six screens: empty brief, brief with plan, failed step, open review,
   adjustment form, result.
2. Manual, in the browser: heading order, landmarks, keyboard order and focus indicator,
   target sizes, form labels and descriptions, reflow at 320 px and at 375 px, motion.
Not done: a pass with a screen reader (VoiceOver, NVDA) and testing with users. What is
listed below as "pass" for assistive tech is read from the markup, not heard.

FINDINGS AND WHAT WAS DONE

A. Contrast of text (1.4.3). Serious. All in design system components.
   1. Primary button: white on #00c73d, 2.27:1.
      Fixed: the kit's text colour on that green, 4.6:1. Hover uses the lighter accent,
      because the darker hover green would drop it to 3.3:1.
   2. Secondary and tertiary buttons: #00c73d text on white, 2.27:1.
      Fixed: text in the kit's text colour; the tertiary button is underlined so it still
      reads as an action without relying on colour.
   3. Info tag: white on #0099de at 12 px, 3.17:1. Error tag: white on #e03430, 4.4:1.
      Fixed: the kit's light info and error backgrounds with dark text and a coloured edge.
   4. Placeholder text: #a5a5a5 on white, 2.4:1.
      Fixed: the kit's muted text colour, 5.7:1.

B. Contrast of non-text elements (1.4.11).
   5. Focus ring: 3 px #00c73d on white, 2.27:1, and invisible on a secondary button,
      whose border is the same green.
      Fixed: 3 px ring in the text colour with a 2 px offset, on every focusable element.
   6. Secondary button border: 2.27:1. Fixed: the kit's darker green, 3.7:1.
   7. Choice chips (our component): #dddddd border, 1.3:1.
      Fixed: muted text colour, 5.7:1; hover keeps a border above 3:1.

C. Structure.
   8. Landmarks (best practice): the evidence panel was a region with the same name once
      per step. Fixed: it is a named group; landmarks are now unique.
   9. Page title (2.4.2): the title did not change between the three stages.
      Fixed: the title starts with the stage ("Ejecución · …").
  10. The sources table had no accessible name. Fixed: named by its heading.

PASSED WITHOUT CHANGES
- Headings: one h1 per stage, no skipped levels.
- Language: lang="es".
- Forms: the text areas and inputs of the kit are labelled and described.
- Keyboard: every control is a native button, summary or field; order follows the page.
- Focus: moves to the main region when the stage changes.
- Target size (2.5.8): no target under 24 by 24 px; chips are 40 px high.
- Reflow (1.4.10): no horizontal scroll at 320 px; the sources table scrolls on its own.
- Use of colour (1.4.1): evidence level, source origin, step status and the current stage
  are written in words, not shown by colour alone.
- Motion: every animation, including the word-by-word text, stops under
  prefers-reduced-motion. The revealed text is complete for assistive tech from the start.
- Status messages (4.1.3): reading the need, the phase of the run, the empty plan and the
  verdict being written are announced through status regions; failures through alerts.

KNOWN LIMITS
- The fixes in section A and B override styles of the design system. They are scoped,
  use only the kit's tokens and are written down as feedback for it, but the right place
  for them is the design system itself.
- A step that opens for review is announced by the status line, but focus stays where it
  was: a keyboard user has to move to the step.
- Error text of the kit's text area is shown by the component; whether it is tied to the
  field for assistive tech was not verified.
- The disabled state of the kit's components was not audited: the interface does not use it.
